The Challenge

Why traditional logistics fails our farmers.

Fragmented Supply

Small farmers cannot fill trucks individually, leading to high per-unit transport costs.

Post-Harvest Loss

Delays in finding transport result in over 30% of fresh produce spoiling before it hits the market.

Exploitative Middlemen

Without direct connections, farmers are forced to sell at rock-bottom prices to intermediaries.

The KUHA Solution

KUHA digitizes the supply chain by aggregating cargo from multiple farmers and matching it with available local drivers.

  • Smart Route Optimization: Reduces fuel consumption by 25%.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Farmers know exactly where their goods are.
  • Instant Booking: Secure transport in minutes, not days.

Core Objectives

What we aim to achieve for the agricultural community.

Connect

Direct link between producers and transporters, cutting out unnecessary layers.

Preserve

Speed up the supply chain to ensure fresh produce reaches the market.

Empower

Give farmers data and control over their logistics, increasing their profit margins.
